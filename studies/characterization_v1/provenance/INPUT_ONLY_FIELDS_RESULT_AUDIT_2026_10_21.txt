What this resolves
------------------
The earlier local-field advantage cannot be attributed solely to revealing the
true category at application: it persists after decoding that category, and
a separately fitted label-free input-only smoother also outperforms ridge in
this excluded-pilot panel. Thus these observations do not support a broad
claim that a global linear operator is the best activation predictor given
the same source activation and action.

What this does not resolve
--------------------------
1. The kernel field retains more training information. It stores 75,264
   displacement coordinates per C7 cell and 49,152 per D4 cell, plus training
   source vectors. These are storage coordinates, not counts of independently
   optimized parameters. A compact map has a different capacity/memory budget.
   No equal-capacity, minimum-description-length or optimality claim is tested.
2. Every kernel cell chooses .125, the narrowest allowed bandwidth. The result
   is a valid fixed-grid comparison, but the grid does not bracket an interior
   optimum. Do not claim a globally optimized kernel or expand the grid using
   pilot performance while presenting the expansion as original confirmation.
3. Both primary ridge and the kernel field here are trained with paired data
   for every group element. This does not test learning only generators and
   predicting actual targets of unseen composites. It does not invalidate the
   historical AFS-A measurements; it identifies a missing strong comparator.
4. Twelve excluded context families from a fixed grammar generator are not
   independent confirmation, an unrestricted language population, or evidence
   of a unique internal mechanism. Low predictive error alone is not a causal
   necessity test. Neither method's result proves that the model memorizes
   categories or lacks algebraic computation.
