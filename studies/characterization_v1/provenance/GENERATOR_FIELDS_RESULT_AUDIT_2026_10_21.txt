Beyond Shared Geometry: generator-only field comparison audit
Artifact series: 2026-10-21 / generator-fields-v1
Status: excluded development pilot; not confirmatory manuscript evidence

Decision
--------
The input-only nonlinear displacement field has lower held-out-composition
prediction error than the tested generator ridge maps in every one of the 48
model/layer/domain cells. Its advantage survives matching the available operation
supervision: both methods fit only r in C7, and only r and s in D4_corner. This
closes an important gap in the preceding all-element field comparison. It does
not establish superiority to every possible group-structured estimator, nor
identify which computation the language model itself implements.

Independent verification
------------------------
An independent NumPy implementation refitted all 48 cells from the exported
float64, 16-dimensional coordinates, without importing the worker's numerical
functions or executing uploaded source. It used normal-equation ridge instead
of the worker's SVD implementation, explicit training-distance enumeration,
direct-difference Gaussian weights, an independent action/target lookup, and
recursive predictions using each method's own intermediate vectors.

Checks cover coordinate/row hashes; finite arrays; unique row IDs; disjoint
train/validation/pilot families; every regularization and bandwidth score;
selected hyperparameters; per-action, per-family and pooled losses; cycles;
matched correct/wrong order controls; and stored coordinate counts. Largest
absolute discrepancy across numerical checks: 9.095e-13.

This verifies refits and scoring conditional on the supplied coordinates and
metadata. It does not independently repeat model forwards, reconstruct PCA from
full hidden states, or certify semantic correctness of the original prompts.

Descriptive results
-------------------
Errors below are squared target-coordinate errors normalized by training
activation energy, averaged across 24 cells per domain. Lower is better.
They are not answer accuracies. Cells share families/models and are not 48
independent experimental replications.

                     Primitive operations       Unseen operation compositions
Domain               Ridge       Kernel         Ridge       Kernel
C7                   0.0118973   0.0039538      0.0247834   0.0060781
D4_corner            0.0479324   0.0288856      0.0595374   0.0345831

Kernel composition error is lower in 24/24 C7 and 24/24 D4_corner cells.
Each domain's 12 family-averaged differences also favors the kernel.
All state categories were seen during training; unseen compositions do not
mean unseen state vocabulary. No true intermediate state, category snapping,
projection, or renormalization is supplied during recursive prediction.

Identity-cycle errors, ridge / kernel:
C7 r^7: 0.0174343 / 0.0002864
D4 r^4: 0.0406675 / 0.0111562
D4 s^2: 0.0199088 / 0.0060374

For both methods, correct-order predictions have lower error than wrong-order
predictions in every D4 cell, in both registered order contrasts. These compare
both predictions to the same correct target and exclude coincident endpoints.
The srs/rsr contrast uses 48 eligible observations per cell; rs/sr uses 96.
Thus cycle and order-sensitive prediction are not exclusive to these linear
maps. These are geometric prediction diagnostics, not causal interventions.

Capacity and selection limitations
---------------------------------
The methods have matched input access and primitive-operation supervision,
but different capacity and storage. Per cell, the ridge matrices store 256
coordinates in C7 and 512 in D4. The kernel stores 21,504 coordinates in C7
and 18,432 in D4 (shared source coordinates plus generator displacements).
These are storage counts, not independent statistical parameter counts.
An equal-memory comparison has not been performed. A compactness/accuracy
tradeoff remains plausible, but has not been established as a contribution.

Every kernel cell selects 0.125, the lowest registered bandwidth. This is a
valid result within the fixed grid, not evidence of an interior/global optimum.
The predictor does not take category labels, but task metadata is still used
to construct paired examples and scoring masks. It is not label-free task
discovery. These are previously inspected pilot families; no fresh test data,
formal hypothesis tests, new calibration, or new model execution is involved.

Next research step
------------------
Do not restart the stopped native/full continuation or weaken its gates.
Do not describe the present results as confirmation of a unique learned
algebraic mechanism or an operator advantage. Preserve the old FULL/AFS
measurements and their narrower scope.

Prepare a new, explicitly pilot-informed characterization study, with a new
study identity and frozen analysis contract before accessing independent test
data. First verify and document whether proposed holdouts have remained unseen.
Include separate all-element and generator-only supervision tracks, the strong
input-only field, prespecified accuracy/storage comparisons and relevant cycle
and order diagnostics. Keep causal intervention claims separate from activation
prediction. Declare family/model dependence, primary estimands and uncertainty
procedures; retain the existing failed/limited-precision power conclusions.

The draft characterization amendment remains a proposal, not an implemented
or approved confirmatory runner. No new GPU command is needed to finish this
audit. Any later test run requires the new contract and implementation review.
