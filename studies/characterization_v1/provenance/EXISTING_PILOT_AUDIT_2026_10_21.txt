What the power output actually says
----------------------------------
C7/D4:
  status = limited_precision_do_not_claim_powered
  selected_families = null
  candidate size = 64 context families
  assumed true advantage = .02; meaningful-superiority margin = .01
  variance inflation = 2; 5,000 simulations per scenario
At the two normal-correlation scenarios, the joint four-endpoint detection
rates are .0264 and .0464. They concern a hypothetical common .02 effect under
the registered variation model, not the probability that the scientific claim
is true or the probability that the paper will be accepted. Observed pilot
means are not .02 for all four outcomes; in particular C7 prediction is negative.
Do not interpret more families as a justified route to a positive joint result.

S3:
  status = power_target_met
  selected_families = 64; selected_orbits = 16
  assumed true advantage = .02; superiority margin = .01
The planning calculation uses measured pilot variance but injects the assumed
.02 mean effect. This is a legitimate conditional power calculation; it does
not say that such an effect was observed. The actual pilot prediction effect
.00045986 and context effect -.00000679 are both much smaller than .01. The
saved high power cannot certify a large benefit, and near-zero point estimates
do not establish equivalence without a fresh evaluation and the appropriate
uncertainty analysis. The crossed variance estimate also comes from only eight
pilot entity triples and remains uncertain.

Decision
--------
1. Preserve the old positive-claim study as stopped. Do not restart the old
   full continuation, change margins, drop weak endpoints, or relabel a power
   flag as an empirical effect.
2. Prepare a separate, explicitly amended study that characterizes predictive
   recovery, category/context alternatives and their limits. Read AMENDMENT_DRAFT
   for endpoints, information conditions, holdout handling and bounded scope.
3. Do not run more native D4 model/prompt searches merely to obtain a pass.
   A native causal branch requires both clean competence and an effective
   computed-state patch carrier, neither supplied by these structural results.
4. Do not add more models before the claim and comparisons are fixed. No
   additional upload is needed to make the present decision.

The historical FULL/AFS recovery measurements remain evidence in their actual
scope. Current results narrow the claim; they do not prove a universal absence
of algebraic computation, a complete category-code explanation, or lack of
value in the project. A characterization paper still needs a distinct and
useful contribution relative to prior work; acceptance is not guaranteed.

No frozen results, published repository, manuscript table, source lock or old
failure was changed. The following proposed amendment remains a draft, not an
executed experiment or confirmation authorization.
