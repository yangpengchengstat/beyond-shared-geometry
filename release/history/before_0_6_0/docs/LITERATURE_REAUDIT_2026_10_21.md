# Primary-source literature reaudit and novelty assessment

Review date: 21 October 2026. This is a targeted, independent reinspection of the ten named closest works and their relevant primary sections. It is not an exhaustive literature search, a line-by-line rereading of every paper, or reproduction of an external experiment. Reported external findings remain the respective authors' findings. The publication claims below use conference records or a conference-labelled primary PDF; arXiv-only works are identified as preprints. Source URLs and the precise inspected versions are recorded below.

## Overall assessment

The strongest defensible contribution is a controlled empirical characterization of what fitted finite actions identify in contextual language-model activations, including their failure to exclude simpler state-code accounts. Recovery of paired linear transformations, cyclic geometry, non-Abelian group computations, composition diagnostics, kernel compatibility, and cross-network comparisons each have substantial precedent. Combining familiar ingredients in a six-model panel is not automatically a sufficient novelty argument.

The newly audited characterization changes the scientific story: it supplies meaningful comparisons that challenge a broad linear-operator advantage. A useful paper could explain *when* state coding, context transport, and storage-limited prediction disagree, using transparent controls and reproducible evidence. The current findings do not yet establish a new native computation mechanism, a universally superior compact predictor, or an advantage over the published causal steering methods. Publication probability cannot be responsibly inferred from reference coverage.

## Seven requested foundational and closest works

### Lenc and Vedaldi — CVPR 2015

**Inspected:** official conference PDF, Sections 2 and 2.1–2.3; transformation learning and network-equivalence formulations. They estimate affine feature transformations from paired transformed inputs, including regularized reconstruction objectives and task-oriented uses, and study network stitching. Thus learning post-hoc linear transport from frozen features is established methodology. Our distinct object is the declared contextual finite-state panel and its diagnostic controls, not the basic fitting principle. Comparing only against an unconditioned displacement does not distinguish our method from this broader transformation-learning tradition. [Primary paper](https://www.cv-foundation.org/openaccess/content_cvpr_2015/papers/Lenc_Understanding_Image_Representations_2015_CVPR_paper.pdf)

### Chughtai, Chan, and Nanda — ICML 2023

**Inspected:** official PMLR PDF, task setup, Section 4's group-composition algorithm, Section 5's logits/weights/activation evidence and ablations. This work analyzes networks trained on binary finite-group composition and explicitly recovers representation matrices and circuit components. Our pretrained LLMs and contextual lexical actions differ from that setting. However, neither non-Abelian neural structure nor representation-based composition can be claimed as newly introduced. External multiplication of fitted matrices also supplies less information about the model's native algorithm than a weight-level computational account. [Primary paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/chughtai23a/chughtai23a.pdf)

### Engels et al. — ICLR 2025

**Inspected:** accepted conference PDF, the contribution/definition framing, Section 5.1, and Appendix C. Calendar circles and causal subspace interventions already appear here. The circular probe maps PCA features to sine/cosine coordinates; the reported intervention also mean-ablates the complementary activation components. Our usual complement-preserving edit is therefore a different intervention condition. A comparison should report both a faithful reproduction and a controlled adaptation under the same preserved-space rule. Statistical feature reducibility in the paper is not interchangeable with invariant-subspace irreducibility. The appendix already discusses finite-group computation, so a blanket claim that earlier calendar work studies geometry alone is inaccurate. [Accepted paper](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/file/d3221cdb27e49d9c1cd35ad254feccfe-Paper-Conference.pdf)

### Nanda et al. — ICLR 2023

**Inspected:** conference-labelled arXiv PDF, Section 3.1, Section 4's four evidence lines, and the abstract's training-progress framing; arXiv record identifies the current file as v3. Fourier/trigonometric modular-addition mechanisms are examined through weights, activations, and ablation. A recovered weekday action in a pretrained model is not the same task or proof of that algorithm. Our contribution should not be described as discovering cyclic computation or explaining how such computation emerges during training: we neither reconstruct the corresponding native circuit nor observe pretraining dynamics. [Primary PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2301.05217v3), [version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.05217v3)

### Yuan Sun — arXiv:2609.18190v1, 2026 preprint

**Inspected:** selected passages in Sections III-B–D and Appendix B's kernel result, Sections V-B–D's comparisons, and Theorem 4's assumptions. This is a direct conceptual neighbor: transformation compatibility, induced composition, kernel preservation, metric-dependent approximation, local state-conditioned transport, and error accumulation are already developed. Its local/global comparisons also address fitting budgets. Our finite-orbit and two-step identities should remain explicitly elementary specializations, not a new general realization theory. The useful remaining distinction is the contextual LLM evaluation and the empirical extent to which category-based controls explain recovery. We should compare assumptions and measured quantities rather than suggest that prior work omitted composition. [Version-specific primary paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.18190v1)

### Wu, Jaburi, Drori, and Gross — ICLR 2025

**Inspected:** arXiv v3, main setup/verified-performance framing and Appendix F; official ICLR publication record. Their analysis relates competing group-operation explanations and obtains quantitative performance guarantees. Appendix F reproduces intervention directions under an algorithmic setting that does not support the proposed coset explanation, demonstrating why such interventions alone are not identifying. Our registration and scramble controls likewise cannot establish a unique native semantic algorithm. This paper supports a discriminating-control framing; it does not validate our particular operator fits. Use the accepted 2025 title and distinguish it from earlier preprint metadata. [Primary v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.07476v3), [official record](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/9a583178bd18b2e55095c3a53f8fe88a-Abstract-Conference.html)

### Alan Sun and Mariya Toneva — ICLR 2026

**Inspected:** arXiv v1 Sections 2 and 5–6, Algorithm 1, stated approximation/slack conditions, and conference-labelled PDF title page. Congruity compares sampled behavior-preserving implementations, rather than interpreting one pairwise similarity or fitted alignment as mechanistic equivalence. Their bounds depend on abstraction/alignment classes and representation quality. Our existing same-element intertwiners do not instantiate this procedure and cannot certify equivalent mechanisms, especially when the fitted alignment can be singular. Alan Sun and Yuan Sun are different authors and must remain separate citations. [Primary HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.30002v1), [conference-labelled primary PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.30002v1)

## Three closest proposed comparison methods

### Arora et al. — arXiv:2609.35970v1, 2026 preprint

**Inspected:** Sections 2.1–2.2, 4–5, and the definitions used for steering. The paper uses a factorial ensemble over three independently varied inputs, separates additive and interaction components, and intervenes using conditional means of relevant interactions. A train-fitted state-centroid control is related but is not a reproduction of that full functional ANOVA analysis. Full comparison requires factorial task construction, declared conditioning variables, and separately fitted interaction means. A simpler matched conditional-mean baseline can be implemented now, but must be labelled an adaptation. Its reported top-k metrics also cannot be silently substituted for our complete-answer accuracy. [Primary v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.35970v1)

### Wurgaft et al. — arXiv:2605.05115v1, 2026 preprint

**Inspected:** selected setup/intervention passages in Section 3 and Appendix A.6. Manifold steering follows fitted intrinsic paths and evaluates behavioral trajectories. Appendix A.6 discloses that manifold edits replace top-64 PCA components while preserving the off-subspace residual, whereas the straight-line baseline replaces the complete activation. Thus the published comparison varies both path geometry and replacement scope. Our comparison must disclose that distinction and include a matched-space control, rather than claim that the published numbers isolate geometry alone. A generic coordinate kernel predictor is not a reproduced manifold-steering method or its behavioral evaluation. [Primary v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.05115v1)

### Hu, Niu, and Varma — arXiv:2607.04525v1, 2026 preprint

**Inspected:** contextual-field definitions and Section 5.1's transport procedure, especially Equation 12 and its evaluation. Target-model test displacements are predicted by combining target training displacements with weights computed from source-model displacement similarities. Computing a test word's source displacement requires its source-model before/after activations. This is a different information condition from editing one supplied activation in a single model. It belongs in a separate cross-model transport track, with held-out target words/contexts and source information fully disclosed. Our current structural single-model comparison does not establish superiority to this procedure. [Primary v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.04525v1)

## Concrete wording and design decisions for this project

These are recommendations derived from the reviewed papers and our own audited design; they are not findings claimed by the external authors.

| Risky wording | Defensible wording or required evidence |
| --- | --- |
| “We introduce recovering transformations from frozen neural representations.” | “We study a specified finite-action recovery protocol in contextual LLM activations.” Cite the established transformation-learning literature. |
| “Previous work concerns geometry, whereas we discover algebra.” | “We evaluate fitted action consistency and prediction under explicit controls.” The prior-work distinction must name the actual task, supervision, and evidence, not erase prior composition studies. |
| “Our theory proves that LLMs learn group semantics.” | “Elementary realizability and perturbation arguments delimit what post-hoc recovery identifies.” |
| “Shared algebra demonstrates a common mechanism across models.” | “Same-element alignments provide a compatibility diagnostic under the stated fit/evaluation protocol.” A stronger interpretation requires a mechanism-specific design. |
| “Operators outperform additive or manifold methods.” | Restrict the claim to named implemented controls on matched metrics. A cancelling global mean is not a representative of conditional fields, manifold steering, or peer methods. |
| “Linear maps are the most compact explanation.” | Report the tested storage/error frontier, including cheap decoded-centroid controls and all construction information. Storage of retained support pairs does not equalize exposure to training target pairs or total memory. |
| “Unseen-composite prediction proves primitive neural composition.” | Distinguish external own-output primitive composition, a direct composite lookup, unseen lexical states, and native model execution. |
| “All peer comparisons are complete.” | Separate available adapters, CPU validation, development pilots, and completed matched empirical comparisons. No external method was executed or reproduced during this literature review. |

For the next experiment, a focused contribution should be selected before expansion. A structural-identification paper needs compact nonlinear/state-code competitors and consistently registered alternatives under explicit information budgets. A causal-steering paper needs clean native competence and matched behavioral comparisons, including intervention location, preserved components, strength selection, full-answer scoring, and failure accounting. A cross-model mechanism paper needs a separate design beyond same-element fitted alignment. Running all branches without a clear central claim would increase cost without resolving these distinctions.

The current completed characterization is suitable for a candid comparative account, including negative and heterogeneous findings. The strongest remaining scientific question is whether any apparent operator benefit survives information-matched state-code and compact nonlinear alternatives, and whether context-transport effects extend beyond the present authored families. The answers must be measured; references and prose cannot supply them. New use of already inspected holdouts is exploratory, and confirmatory claims require a new frozen evaluation contract and fresh evaluation units.
