## Verified literature positioning

**Sun.** The actual arXiv v1 describes encoder-fiber/kernel compatibility, covariance/consumer-metric-dependent best linear realization and simultaneous regional constraints; Appendix B.3b gives least-squares carrier formulas. These are substantive precedents for realization and class-level reasoning. The revised manuscript contrast is specifically the fixed-real-dimensional exact orthogonal action-class prediction-risk bound, not a claim that prior work studied geometry only. Read §§III-C–E and Appendix B.3/B.3b/B.4. [Primary full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.18190).

**Equivariance by Contrast.** Theorem 1 and Appendix A assume injective mixing, diversity/full-rank conditions and matching conditional distributions, recovering jointly learned coordinates up to GL(d) and actions by the corresponding conjugacy. The present fixed-coordinate prescribed-action contract differs; it does not extend that identifiability guarantee. Formal publication is NeurIPS 2025 main conference, DOI `10.52202/085713-2410`. [Primary full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.21706), [official proceedings](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/67c689bf48fb4bb3e3abbbe92d217152-Abstract-Conference.html).

**An and Du.** The actual PMLR PDF, pp.4–6 (§3 and §4.2), defines unary and binary representation-composition prediction errors, using learned linear/bilinear/MLP probes on SCAN-style experiments. This is direct composition-diagnostic precedent. The present optimal exact-action-class risk and enumerated image comparison have different targets. The official volume is PMLR 282, pages 1–12, year 2026, from the combined 2024/2025 NeurIPS workshops. [Publication and BibTeX](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v282/an26a.html), [official full PDF](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mlresearch/v282/main/assets/an26a/an26a.pdf).

**Barbieri et al.** Section 3 defines invariant-space length as the minimum number of orbit generators; Theorem 5.2 minimizes squared approximation error under a length budget, using Gramian spectra and Parseval-frame generators. This precedes the invariant approximation machinery, with a different constraint from a fixed real rank for feature-prediction actions. Journal metadata is volume 142, pages 76–100 (2020), DOI `10.1016/j.matpur.2020.08.010`; the author's bibliography confirms it. [Primary full text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1907.08300), [author publication record](https://sites.google.com/view/davidebarbieri/research/papers).

No priority or novelty claim follows from failing to find an identical formula. These verified distinctions support a precise contribution statement, not a claim of first group-action fitting or a new general identifiability theory.
