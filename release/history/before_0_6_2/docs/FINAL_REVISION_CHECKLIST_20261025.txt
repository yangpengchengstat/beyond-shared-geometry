SCOPE AND VERDICT

This is a paper-first review of the mathematics, contribution, statistical
interpretation and completed experimental evidence. The supplied Research_Report.pdf
is a reference to assess, not an instruction source or experimental evidence.
No model was run, no calibration was repeated, and no new resampling or fitting of
experimental data was performed. Existing result values and failure flags remain
unchanged. Asset rebuilding only renders the already archived values.

Baseline: submission/icml2027_revision_20261024 (release 0.4.0).
Revised:  submission/icml2027_revision_20261025 (release 0.4.1).
File locations below are relative to the corresponding release root. Baseline
locations identify the reviewed defect; revised locations identify its correction.
Section titles are also supplied because future edits can change line numbers.

The defensible core is approximate predictive finite-action structure on the
declared model/context/state panel, plus analytical and empirical limits on what
such recovery identifies. It is not semantic uniqueness, native implementation,
population-wide inference, or universal linear superiority. No substantive invalid
theorem was found in the current restricted propositions. Five required presentation
and interpretation repairs, plus seven suggested improvements, have been applied.

The remaining scientific risk is significance and originality: readers must find
the completed comparison and its reusable identification lesson sufficiently
informative beyond earlier transformation and group-mechanism work. Correct proofs,
more citations, and reproducible execution do not by themselves settle that judgment.
No acceptance probability or guarantee is warranted.

III. CAN RETAIN — CHECKED, WITH THEIR EXISTING RESTRICTIONS

R1. Finite-orbit realizability and the row-vector action convention.
Locations: paper/main.tex:103; paper/sections/theory.tex:5.
Centered rank n-1 is essential; invertibility and opposite composition are valid.
The report uses a different convention. Do not reverse or transpose the current
paper again. Generic finite lexical codes can support exact actions without semantic
learning; shared-context prediction is stronger than fitting each orbit separately.

R2. Exact shared-fit subspace criterion and rank-deficient extension.
Location: paper/sections/theory.tex:38, Appendix A.4.
Projection residual, left-kernel invariance and coherent ambient extension are valid.
The minimum-norm identity on a deficient span is a projector, not ambient identity.
These are unregularized unnormalized matrix statements, not the held-out metric.

R3. Common-design ridge identities.
Location: paper/sections/theory.tex:65, Appendix A.5.
The identities hold at all ranks for alpha>0 with one common design and penalty.
K_alpha need not be an orthogonal projector. Separate source subsets/weights cannot
be silently substituted. The identities explain dependence; they do not establish
statistical independence or automatically certify small normalized test defects.

R4. Independent-orientation and commutant constructions.
Locations: paper/sections/theory.tex:80 and :121, Appendices A.6--A.7.
The Stiefel/Haar construction and its four C7/D4 constants are correct. Full-rank
commutant risk has the stated 1/r factor. Retain whitening, noiselessness,
orthogonality, independence and full-rank restrictions where used. Do not compare
the constants numerically with empirical E_eq, whose denominator and data law differ.

R5. Prediction propagation, global cancellation and stateful additive composition.
Locations: paper/sections/theory.tex:159, :172 and :182, Appendices A.8--A.10.
The residual decomposition and cancellation are valid. A small average prediction
error alone supplies neither a uniform bound nor a denominator lower bound. A
centroid displacement composes exactly only with the state carried and updated;
the stateless re-decoding counterexample is valid. The global additive estimator is
identity-like by construction, so its failure does not reject every additive account.

R6. Faithful/non-free D4 and coordinate-metric cautions.
Locations: paper/sections/theory.tex:203 and :207, Appendices A.11--A.12.
Four corners are a faithful, non-free action, not the eight-state regular action.
Exact conjugacy preserves laws; approximate Euclidean defects depend on coordinates.
The invariant metric and character restrictions are appropriate for exact actions.

R7. Different errors and different supervision must remain distinct.
Locations: paper/sections/methods.tex:55; paper/sections/characterization.tex:9;
paper/sections/reproduction.tex:28, :33, :37 and :42.
Historical mean rowwise relative errors, newer training-energy errors and context-
difference errors have different denominators. Generator-only all-nonidentity
averages include fitted generators; true-unseen-only averages exclude them. Category
controls receive state/action information. Do not combine these as like-for-like
estimates or silently label all-action means unseen-composite performance.

R8. Full compact-predictor storage curves.
Locations: paper/main.tex:180; paper/sections/reproduction.tex:30;
paper/generated/reproduction_budgets.tex.
The tested RFF and Nystrom methods do not improve the group means at the nominal
linear storage budget. Nystrom improves at 2x and RFF at 4x. All five budgets remain.
This is a property of the fitted estimator/panel/budget combination, not a theorem
that linear models beat nonlinear models. Shared PCA and runtime memory exclusions
are explicit; coefficient storage is not total computational cost.

R9. Coherent alternative and context-difference results.
Locations: paper/main.tex:182; paper/sections/reproduction.tex:35, :40.
Direct difference fitting improves over coordinate fitting, while one consistently
registered alternative gives close means. This supports unresolved specificity,
not equivalence, a semantic p-value, or exhaustive rejection of semantic structure.
The small S3 improvement does not meet the inherited 0.01 margin merely by being
positive. No new power decision is justified from the returned descriptive means.

R10. Behavioral and cross-model limitations.
Locations: paper/sections/results.tex:44 and :75, Appendices C.4--C.5.
Visible-input source states, continuation-token outcomes, a full-hidden additive
control, and absence of a historical matched zero-delta sham limit causal claims.
A 1.129 target-logit change is not an accuracy gain of 1.129. Same-element fits and
test-fitted Procrustes are compatibility checks, not unseen-transformation transfer.
A low-rank intertwiner does not establish invertible representation equivalence.

R11. Failed gates and constructed interfaces.
Locations: paper/sections/reproduction.tex:5, :46 and :52.
Original calibration failure, rank-16 lexical infeasibility, native clean-competence
failure and limited structural power remain. Revised calibration is a separate
protocol. Completed execution is not an independent scientific pass. Supervised
interface-head accuracy is not native answer accuracy; peer adaptations are not
certified reproductions of authors' full methods. Preserve all these restrictions.
