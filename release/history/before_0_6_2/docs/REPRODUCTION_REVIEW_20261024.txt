Claim ledger

1. Historical finite-action prediction and generator tests are computationally
   reproducible. Comparisons of 1,193 rows across five prior tables differ by
   at most about 4.44e-15 in the compared estimates/endpoints. They are replays
   of inspected designs, not 1,193 independent replications.
2. Five representative complete coordinate analyses agree at rtol=1e-7 and
   atol=1e-9, with maximum absolute difference about 1.42e-6 over 173,314
   numeric leaves. Raw model forwards/PCA and all neural training fits were
   not independently rerun during this local audit. Equality is not bitwise.
3. At unit nominal storage, the tested compact nonlinear predictors do not
   beat ridge on average. Larger budgets matter: the full curves retain
   improvements by Nystrom at 2x and RFF at 4x in both groups. This is neither
   universal linear superiority nor universal nonlinear superiority.
4. Category means provide a cheap competitive C7 predictor but receive
   construction labels and the known action table. Logical float64/int64
   parameter bytes exclude common PCA, weights and temporary runtime memory.
   Equal storage is not equal statistical capacity or equal information.
5. Context-difference fitting improves C7/D4 mean transport relative to using
   coordinate-fit operators. Comparable coherent swap01 action results leave
   semantic specificity unresolved. One coherent reference is not a semantic
   permutation p-value. S3 improvements are small in absolute units.
6. Supervised interface group regularization does not improve C7/D4 average
   target prediction over reconstruction. S3 transported-state classification
   is weak. This is a constructed head, not native LM answer accuracy or a
   recovered internal computation. Each domain/arm averages 24 cells and
   three technical seeds; seeds do not supply new datasets.
7. Peer implementations are adaptations. Exact author code/prompt/layer
   equivalence is not certified. Factorial zero-delta native answer accuracy
   is low. These branches cannot support a SOTA or competent-task causal
   superiority claim. Different-target sham/edit scores cannot be subtracted
   as though the correct answer were unchanged.
8. Hu-style word transport receives source test displacements. Structural
   intertwiners are weak predictive maps on this panel. Product actions add
   coverage while the local displacement comparator is substantially better.
   Their information regimes and endpoints must remain separate.
9. Original calibration failed, rank-16 lexical D was infeasible, clean native
   competence failed, structural power remained limited, and both confirmation
   branches remain blocked. Separate revised calibration/rank-aware D records
   do not overwrite those outcomes. Completion counts describe execution only.
10. The theory report's ridge, orientation and commutant identities are added
    with proofs and restricted hypotheses. They are analytical boundaries,
    not new LLM observations or a priority claim. No Haar-null significance,
    noisy recovery theorem or native mechanism certificate is asserted.

Endpoint dictionary

- Historical E_eq: mean per-row squared target-relative error in the frozen
  study. Historical operator-space defects instead use unsquared Frobenius
  relative norms. Their magnitudes are not directly comparable.
- Compact nmse: equal-action mean over nonidentity actions, with changed-state
  rows. The generator_only track fits primitives but its exported all-action
  mean includes those primitives. Do not label that mean unseen-composite-only.
  The retained per-action outputs can separately describe excluded elements.
- Context nmse: paired paraphrase-difference prediction, with a training-only
  difference-energy denominator. It is not compact coordinate-prediction loss.
- Interface target loss: full-hidden target-prediction loss with its own
  training-energy denominator. Interface classification comes from the learned
  supervised state head; it is not token generation accuracy.
- Cross-model error: row-normalized transport error, with declared test-source
  information. Product error has its own conditional task and normalization.

No new p-values, bootstrap intervals, power decisions, parameter tuning or
test-selected budget claims are introduced by rendering these results. Fixed
model/readout means describe this selected panel, not a population of models.

Preservation and future runtime

The old frozen source/configuration/results and source manifests are retained.
scripts/restore_frozen_source.py reconstructs the recorded numerical source
and launcher inputs into a new directory and verifies all bytes; it never
launches experiments. Its restored old behavior includes the known rank and
directory failures. The new maintenance runtime instead has a versioned fits
directory fix, atomic parameter/payload validation, per-recipient checkpoints,
explicit progress and durable attempt timings. Its default is check-only.
The underlying objectives, metrics, model pins, seeds and gates are unchanged.

The manuscript and Overleaf bundle use the unmodified ICML 2026 fallback.
Official ICML 2027 requirements remain unverified. CPU checks, source checks,
compilation and visual inspection establish delivery quality within that
scope, not conference acceptance or an independently confirmed mechanism.

Next scientific decision

Use the completed evidence to evaluate the narrowed characterization claim.
A future native-mechanism study would need an excluded-data development step,
a competent frozen interface and fresh held-out families with paired strong
baselines. It should answer one falsifiable question; it should not reuse
inspected test outcomes for tuning and then claim independent confirmation.
No such model run or additional calibration is scheduled in this revision.
