SUBSTANTIVE MANUSCRIPT CHANGES
1. Main Sections 1-2: one shared family acts in one fixed representation space
   across contexts without context-specific realignment. The labeled action is
   given. Distinguish fitted risk, class-optimal risk, supported-space action
   parameters and the unobserved native mechanism. Exact shared prediction is
   compatible with a stable state code.
2. Appendix A: the shared-map least-squares criterion is unrestricted in operator
   norm and does not establish orthogonal or norm-capped feasibility. Unequal
   ambient extensions outside observed support alone do not prove incompatibility.
3. Main Section 3.2 and Appendix B.3: distinguish total real-type energy e_tau
   from budget-selected energy b_tau. Their sums are 1 and gamma, respectively.
   Selected allocations can depend on tied optima. State-invariant/trivial energy
   is not a pure-context measurement; category coding remains a possible account.
   No directions are deleted, and no empirical type energies are asserted.
4. Main Section 4.3: a nonlinear predictor shared across contexts is not thereby
   an invertible action satisfying group laws. The existing capacity comparison
   remains a prediction comparison, with original storage definitions and scores.
5. Main Section 4.4 and the context-difference appendix: same-state differences
   remove the state main effect but can retain the context main effect. A short
   marginal-mean decomposition makes this explicit. Difference prediction does
   not isolate state-context interactions, and a nonlinear difference map need
   not be induced by an action on the original coordinates.
6. Appendix B.4 and Discussion: a complete observed bank can define an empirical
   distribution for deterministic bounds in its second-moment metric. It is not
   additional centering, a historical held-out score, or a new-context certificate.
   Numerical rank/conditioning failures do not justify silently changing r.
7. Preserve the Theorem 7/8 statements and proofs, type-gap scale restriction,
   D4 four-corner support check, AFS-B restricted-reference interpretation,
   AFS-C refit/true-target distinction, and registration-specific scoring masks.
   No old finding is relabeled as an independent confirmation.
