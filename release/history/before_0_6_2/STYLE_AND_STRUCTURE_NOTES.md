# Writing examples: bounded lessons for v0.5.9

This is an editorial note about structure and prose, not an assessment of the example papers' scientific merit. The review covered all 18 supplied PDFs in `TMLR_accepted_eamples`: 17 distinct manuscripts, because the two LRanker files are byte-identical. Main-text heading hierarchies, introduction and result passages, captions, and appendix divisions were inspected. No sample experiment was reproduced. Page references below are PDF pages.

**Material correction.** MoEFL is present: *Global Motif Embedding Meet Few-Shot Graph Learning* (`8472_Global_Motif_Embedding_Me.pdf`) names the method on page 2. The earlier filename-based absence statement was incorrect; its note is preserved in the release history. A separate per-paper inventory records the inspected passages for all 17 manuscripts.

## Concrete lessons used in this bounded revision

| Editorial action | Examples and locations | Application to BSG v0.5.9 |
|---|---|---|
| Move from an intelligible problem to the target and contribution. | SAT pp. 1–3 moves from the adaptation problem to a motivating illustration and formulation. MoEFL pp. 1–4 introduces the missing structural information before its components. LRanker pp. 2–4 separates candidate scale from test-time computation. | Preserve the weekday example and three parallel targets. Define what the bound measures before interpreting it; make the fixed metric and action class explicit rather than relying on the adjective “low-risk.” |
| Organize results around a question, then give the comparison, observation and interpretation. | *Weight Clustering* §3.2 (pp. 9–10) introduces an alternative explanation before its control. *From Images to Signals* §4.1 (p. 6) groups the evaluation by questions; §4.4 ends with concise insights (p. 13). | Keep the v0.5.8 question-led structure. Tighten transitions and state the conclusion supported by each comparison instead of reporting the order in which studies were run. |
| Follow numbers with their meaning and the qualification that changes that meaning. | *Towards Autoregressive Neural Network Parameter Generation* p. 11 distinguishes an aggregate advantage from a single-task contribution. *Uncertainty Quantification for LLM Function-Calling* pp. 7, 12 separates discrimination, calibration and abstention. | Keep C7 concentration and heterogeneous orthogonality contrasts next to their summaries. Distinguish an average from a uniform effect; retain the separation between class risk, constructed-action transfer and registration budget. Do not soften inconvenient conditions. |
| Make captions sufficient to read the comparison, and let the prose explain why it matters. | *Separable Pathways* Table 6 (p. 16) links components, mechanisms and evidence. *Minerva* p. 10 states the unit of uncertainty next to results. MoEFL Figure 1 (p. 4) distinguishes preprocessing, training and testing. | Keep the metric, population of rows, direction and object of comparison visible. Avoid turning captions into a second discussion or allowing a summary table to imply identical metrics. |
| Use appendices for depth, not to finish an incomplete main-text argument. | SAT separates main formulation from implementation detail; LRanker separates formulation from additional comparisons; *SAGE* Table 10 (p. 18) identifies component provenance before referring to technical records. | Keep definitions, closest-work positioning and the interpretation in the main text. Retain proof, complete records and reproducibility links where needed; remove navigation that merely repeats that more material exists. |

The remaining examples—UNO, MeritKV, Reflection or Re-Generation, AI University, CausalPlan, VRPAgent and Energy-Based Attribute Models—reinforce the usefulness of separating setup from outcome, grouping controls by their purpose, and giving limitations a clear local or concluding home. Their different section orders and lengths also show why no single outline should be copied.

This pass borrows these writing actions, not sample sentences, superiority claims, figures, citation quotas or page limits. The existing narrative, numerical findings, C7 heterogeneity, failed branches and unexecuted LOFO status remain intact. The samples are neither a TMLR formatting rule nor evidence of this manuscript's acceptance probability; they do not justify new experiments or stronger claims.
