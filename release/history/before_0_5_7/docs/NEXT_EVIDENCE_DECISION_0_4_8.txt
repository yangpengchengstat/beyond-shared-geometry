Evidence decision after manuscript revision 0.4.8
Date: 28 October 2026
Status: recommendations only; no new empirical analysis or experiment executed

CURRENT POSITION
The paper's retained analytical and fixed-panel descriptive claims do not require
a new experiment merely to become logically supportable. The revision clarifies
these claims; it does not produce the missing LLM spectral results or upgrade
failed scientific branches into passes. It does not establish an acceptance
probability, new literature priority, independent generalization or native use.

NEXT STEP: ONE BOUNDED ANALYSIS OF EXISTING COORDINATES
The read-only inventory now establishes a local data basis: 48 C7/D4 cells have
saved coordinates and paired PCA/row metadata, with 128 complete test state
blocks per cell. This advances the availability question posed in 0.4.7; it does
not establish numerical eligibility. Original archive/member hashes, selected
bank identity, empirical full rank and stable whitening remain prerequisites.

The next useful evidence task is the retrospective finite-bank diagnostic in
SPECTRAL_FINITE_BANK_PROTOCOL_0_4_8.txt. Its purpose is to connect Theorem 7 to
the observed representation bank and measure what the type-constrained budget
adds beyond regression error and ordinary spectrum. It includes total and
budget-selected type energies, especially state-invariant/trivial energy.

No new LLM forward pass, model download, RunPod rental, full replay or calibration
is needed if the saved inputs pass. Missing or unstable inputs produce an explicit
not_evaluable record. Do not automatically recreate them with model execution.
The proposed protocol is not implemented or executed by the manuscript revision;
it requires its own finalized source identity and a fresh output location.

1. Verify input identities and finalize the already-observed bank and engineering

2. Implement and review the small CPU analysis with analytic correctness checks;

No runtime or cost estimate is promised before implementation and input checks.
The matrices are small and the saved coordinate members can be read selectively;
the local archive need not be fully expanded. No real-bank computation was
performed to prepare this recommendation.

WHEN TO ADD AN INDEPENDENT CONTEXT EVALUATION
If the paper remains a characterization of fixed finite-state panels, defer it.
If robustness across an independently designed context construction becomes a
core claim, a focused independent-context evaluation is needed. The finite-bank
risk bounds, even if informative, do not replace that evidence.

A defensible follow-up would freeze independently authored context families,
paired variants when testing context-difference transport, and complete state
blocks before model outputs are inspected. It must not merely recycle or
paraphrase the original templates. State vocabulary remains seen unless a
separate unseen-state design is explicitly specified.

Freeze model revisions, readouts, coordinates, fitting/selection rules and one
primary comparison. Prefer existing frozen predictors. If a new training portion
is needed, separate it from the final independent test bank in advance. Do not
select layers, budgets or registrations on the final test results. Dedicated
context-difference fitting versus coordinate-fitted operators on the same paired
differences is a suitable candidate primary contrast; the retained scientific
claim must determine the final choice before execution.

Use paired uncertainty at independently authored context-family level, with a
predeclared practical effect and multiplicity policy. Derive sample size from
precision/power for that contrast, not from model/layer/split counts or the
conservative Theorem 8 precheck. The approximately 17,000-context thresholds in
that theorem are neither recommended experimental sizes nor intrinsic lower
bounds. No new sample size or GPU experiment is committed by this revision.

RESEARCH SCOPE
Do not broaden groups, models or ranks to obtain a positive result. Do not
restart failed native interventions simply to add a positive causal component.
Retain original calibrations, rank failures, scientific stops, eligibility flags
and every previous publication. Better scientific positioning comes from a
specific supported contribution, not from the volume of rerun experiments.
