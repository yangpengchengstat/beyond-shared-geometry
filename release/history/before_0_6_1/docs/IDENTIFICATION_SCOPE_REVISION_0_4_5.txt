specification. The empirical findings and all numerical values are unchanged.

exact action uniqueness on observed support, and native implementation. A fit
is a witness under its conditions; selected fitted candidates do not exhaust
an entire predictor class.

in fixed coordinates; unobserved complementary action remains chosen, and
new contexts can add constraints. Retain the existing no-population-inference
qualification and the existing proof, without a new proposition.

semantic relabeling changes the registered task when studying preference.

its global linear/conjugacy equivalence and assumptions. Explicitly credit
