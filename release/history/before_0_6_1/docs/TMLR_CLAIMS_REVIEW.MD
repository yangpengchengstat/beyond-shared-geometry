# Claims and manuscript integration audit

Review date: 30 October 2026. Scope: release 0.5.0 theory, follow-up protocols, context-bank construction, and extension numerical definitions. No experiments or manuscript changes were performed. Numerical outcomes and archive integrity are reviewed separately.

## Assessment

The fixed-dimensional spectral theorem is internally coherent under its stated observation contract. The key risk is integration that silently changes its metric, action class, or scope. A completed run is not a positive scientific decision. Completed but unevaluable endpoints should replace pending slots with explicit failures; they do not require inventing successful outcomes or repairing the frozen protocol.

The strongest coherent paper is about the difference between predictive recovery, compatibility with an exact action, and semantic identification. Empirical spectral and witness results should now connect the theorem to the measured representations. New-context results should answer a narrower robustness question about the training objective. Avoid turning the paper into a collection of unrelated success claims.

It is not a lower bound for arbitrary linear/GL predictors or historical Ridge/Joint. |

## Critical empirical interpretation constraints

1. **Keep the two spectral metrics visibly separate.** `studies/spectral_extensions_v1/numerics.py::witness_comparison` fits whitening, invariant selection, alignment and all operators on old training blocks. Its old-test losses use that frozen training metric. `alternative_comparison` additionally computes a descriptive spectrum using the test bank's own second moment. Those test spectral bounds cannot bound the reported frozen-metric test witness loss. Use separate table panels or explicit column groups. A difference between their values is not a theorem violation or a generalization premium. On the training bank, a valid witness can optionally tighten the displayed upper bound to its attained risk, if labeled as a constructive upper bound rather than the analytic endpoint.

2. **The frozen ridge comparator is exceptionally simple in these coordinates.** Training whitening gives `X'X/N = I`, so the extension's fixed-alpha ridge is OLS divided by `1 + 0.001`, up to numerical error. Do not interpret small OLS/ridge differences as an extensive regularization comparison or as representative of raw-coordinate ridge. This follows directly from the frozen code; it does not require a new experiment.

3. **Alternative registrations compare tasks with different target correspondences.** Retain all 120 C7 and three D4 image subgroups, distinguish image subgroups from generator descriptions/homomorphisms, and report semantic rank with ties. The full action average is insensitive to reindexing by group automorphisms. Enumeration yields descriptive compatibility, not a permutation p-value or a posterior probability of the semantic action. Rank by gamma is a ranking of that summary, not necessarily of exact `L*` or test prediction. If endpoints fail, distinguish the number with usable test spectra from the number with usable witnesses; one cannot stand in for the other.

4. **Mechanism controls are illustrations, not calibration.** M1 and M3 admit shared actions before noise; M3 is deliberately an oblique state/context direct sum. M2 draws context-dependent orientations, but the label alone is not a proof that a particular finite instance has no shared action. Do not describe M3 as a negative null, present M2 detection as a false-positive rate/power estimate, or claim that the summaries distinguish semantic computation from state codes. Near-zero M1/M3 values and their numerical failures deserve separate treatment; machine-scale failures need not contradict exact analytical realizability.

5. **Describe context novelty from the executed bank, not the protocol shorthand.** `bank.py:89` uses 64 assistant-authored paired outer frames shared across both domains, with fixed new clauses “The scheduled weekday is [state]” and “The marked square corner is [state]”. However, `src/bsg_next/data.py:137–139` and the retained characterization source use old clauses “the designated weekday is [state]” and “the marked corner is [state]”. The factual wording therefore changes too; the new clauses are fixed **within the new bank**, not unchanged from the historical bank. Record this discrepancy explicitly and correct claims that *only* the outer narrative changed. The experiment jointly changes the outer narrative and this limited clause wording, so it cannot isolate their separate effects. State vocabularies remain seen. At the causal decoder's target-token span, post-target suffixes cannot influence the activation. This is not evaluation on arbitrary new grammars, unseen concepts, or independently sampled human language. The two variants and both domains share a family identity; they are not extra independent samples.

6. **The primary context comparison is fairly matched for selection, but narrow.** Both training objectives select each action's penalty on the same old-validation context-difference loss and grid. Keep the coordinate-validation-selected arm secondary, and state that both fit all training states while scoring changed states and equally weighted nonidentity actions. The main endpoint is paired context-difference transport, not ordinary coordinate prediction or exact-action consistency. Effects use the old-training difference energy, so magnitudes need not be bounded by one and should not be compared with whitened spectral risks.

7. **Statistical claims stay descriptive.** The primary context estimate averages model/readout cells within domain, then the two domains, producing 64 family effects. Its 2,000-draw bootstrap keeps all repeated states/actions/cells/domains together. Label the interval a family-resampling sensitivity range conditional on this authored bank and fixed panel. It does not include training/refitting uncertainty, model population variation, or independently sampled human prose. Preserve the primary sign convention (difference minus coordinate; negative favors difference fitting), report domain heterogeneity, and do not make new significance/equivalence claims or count 48 cells as independent trials.

8. **Review attestation has a limited meaning.** `run.py::review` creates an explicit operator attestation naming the bank/source hashes; it is not an authenticated human signature or an independent semantic audit. Preserve the received record and its actual timing. A final manuscript may state recorded review, but must not infer independent human authorship or a broader review than the attestation specifies. No new review should be fabricated retroactively.

## TMLR implications

TMLR prioritizes supported claims and clear communication of findings of interest. Its criteria explicitly allow reducing claims when evidence is insufficient; neither state-of-the-art performance nor strong novelty is necessary. Therefore unfavorable or numerically incomplete follow-ups can still support a useful, carefully delimited paper. The paper should explain what a representation researcher learns from the controls, not advertise the number of completed stages. [Official acceptance criteria](https://jmlr.org/tmlr/acceptance-criteria.html), checked 30 October 2026.

The official author guide requires an anonymous, complete PDF using the TMLR style. It imposes no fixed page limit, but length should be justified; appendices are read at reviewer discretion. Keep the main mathematical target and decisive results in the main text. Supplementary material must be anonymous PDF/ZIP and at most 100 MB. Do not package private coordinate caches or the full raw run merely to demonstrate completeness. [Official author guide](https://jmlr.org/tmlr/author-guide.html), checked 30 October 2026.

The overlap policy excludes expanded archival conference papers, while permitting preprints/nonarchival work. An ICML working draft is not itself evidence of prior archival submission. Verify actual publication/submission history and final authorship separately; LLM assistance does not transfer author responsibility. These checks are not supplied by execution receipts. [Official editorial policies](https://jmlr.org/tmlr/editorial-policies.html), checked 30 October 2026.
