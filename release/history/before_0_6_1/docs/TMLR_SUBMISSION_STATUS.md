## Official guidance checked

TMLR bases acceptance on supported claims and clear findings of interest. Claims
may be narrowed when evidence is insufficient; strong novelty and benchmark
state of the art are not requirements. The completed mixed findings therefore
remain part of the paper rather than reasons to change the frozen analysis.
[Acceptance criteria](https://jmlr.org/tmlr/acceptance-criteria.html).

Submissions must use the official style and be anonymous and complete. There is
no fixed page limit, but length should be justified. Anonymous supplementary
material must be PDF or ZIP and no larger than 100 MB.
[Author guidelines](https://jmlr.org/tmlr/author-guide.html).

Check actual archival publication or concurrent submission history before
submission. A retained ICML working draft alone is not a record of archival
publication or submission. All authors remain responsible for the final content,
including material produced with LLM assistance.
[Editorial policies](https://jmlr.org/tmlr/editorial-policies.html).
