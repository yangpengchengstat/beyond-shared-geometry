# Requested prior works and methodological anchors

Verified primary versions were reviewed during the project audit and the targeted follow-up search, with an evidence cutoff of 16 October 2026. The table distinguishes bibliographic identity from our assessment of the proposed contribution. External experiments were not independently reproduced. Follow the linked originals rather than a secondary summary.

| Work | Primary version | Constraint on our claim |
|---|---|---|
| Karel Lenc and Andrea Vedaldi, *Understanding Image Representations by Measuring Their Equivariance and Equivalence* | [CVPR 2015 record](https://www.cv-foundation.org/openaccess/content_cvpr_2015/html/Lenc_Understanding_Image_Representations_2015_CVPR_paper.html); [full paper](https://www.cv-foundation.org/openaccess/content_cvpr_2015/papers/Lenc_Understanding_Image_Representations_2015_CVPR_paper.pdf). Cite 2015 for this conference paper, not the 2014 preprint date. | Learning representation transport and comparing equivariance/equivalence are established. Use reconstruction and downstream effects as distinct evaluations. |
| Bilal Chughtai, Lawrence Chan and Neel Nanda, *A Toy Model of Universality: Reverse Engineering how Networks Learn Group Operations* | [ICML 2023, PMLR 202:6243–6267](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/chughtai23a.html). | Representation-theoretic group-operation mechanisms already have detailed precedents. Our frozen pretrained LLM setting differs, but that difference needs a substantive empirical finding. |
| Joshua Engels, Eric J. Michaud, Isaac Liao, Wes Gurnee and Max Tegmark, *Not All Language Model Features Are One-Dimensionally Linear* | [ICLR 2025 record](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/d3221cdb27e49d9c1cd35ad254feccfe-Abstract-Conference.html); [full paper](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/file/d3221cdb27e49d9c1cd35ad254feccfe-Paper-Conference.pdf). | Calendar circles, causal calendar patches and finite-group discussion are prior work. Statistical irreducibility is distinct from group-representation irreducibility. |
| Neel Nanda, Lawrence Chan, Tom Lieberum, Jess Smith and Jacob Steinhardt, *Progress measures for grokking via mechanistic interpretability* | [ICLR 2023 primary record](https://openreview.net/forum?id=9XFSbDPmdW); [arXiv paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.05217). | Fourier/circuit explanations of modular arithmetic are established; operator fit alone offers less mechanistic evidence than a traced computation. |
| Wilson Wu, Louis Jaburi, Jacob Drori and Jason Gross, *Towards a Unified and Verified Understanding of Group-Operation Networks* | [ICLR 2025 proceedings](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/9a583178bd18b2e55095c3a53f8fe88a-Abstract-Conference.html); [arXiv version](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.07476). | Group-operation mechanisms have alternative implementations and formal analyses. Do not equate an accurate action abstraction with a uniquely identified implementation. |
| Yuan Sun, *Transformation Laws in Neural Representations: Structure, Realisability, and Construction* | [arXiv:2609.18190v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18190v1); [full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.18190v1). Conference acceptance not verified. | Action realisability, prediction versus transport/composition, local/global realizations and error propagation substantially narrow a generic action-recovery novelty claim. |
| Alan Sun and Mariya Toneva, *Tracking Equivalent Mechanistic Interpretations Across Neural Networks* | [ICLR 2026 primary forum](https://openreview.net/forum?id=9lycwRxAOI); [arXiv:2603.30002v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.30002v1). | Congruity compares implementation families under declared assumptions. A potentially singular same-element intertwiner is a different, narrower compatibility measurement. |

Two additional patching sources determine required controls:

- **Aleksandar Makelov, Georg Lange, Atticus Geiger and Neel Nanda**, *Is This the Subspace You Are Looking for? An Interpretability Illusion for Subspace Activation Patching*, [ICLR 2024 proceedings](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/70b8505ac79e3e131756f793cd80eb8d-Abstract-Conference.html). A successful subspace patch can exploit a dormant pathway; success alone does not identify native use. The accepted version has four authors.
- **Fred Zhang and Neel Nanda**, *Towards Best Practices of Activation Patching in Language Models: Metrics and Methods*, [ICLR 2024 proceedings](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/06a52a54c8ee03cd86771136bc91eb1f-Abstract-Conference.html); [full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2309.16042v2). Corruption choice, scoring and intervention granularity affect conclusions. Use natural counterfactuals and declared complete-answer metrics while retaining raw margin diagnostics.
