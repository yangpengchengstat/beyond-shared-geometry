Findings-focused manuscript revision - release 0.4.4

Scope and changes
-----------------
This is a publication-only revision of 0.4.3. It changes the abstract,
contribution paragraph and conclusion to lead with three observed findings:
predictive recovery with viable alternatives; storage-dependent predictor
rankings; and improved context transport after direct difference fitting,
with a tested coherent registration giving close descriptive means.
The main table is reordered to emphasize these comparisons. Its 46 numerical
values, selectors and input hashes are unchanged. Repeated global inference
limits are consolidated in the protocol; comparison-specific supervision,
normalization, budget, target and causal restrictions remain beside results.

Evaluation-grid correction
--------------------------
The previous phrase "same source/action grid" did not distinguish the shared
source bank from registration-specific changed-state scoring masks. Each
registration uses its own targets. D4 conjugacy preserves fixed-point counts
but can change their identities, hence the retained source/action pairs.
Within one registration the compared predictors still share targets and masks.
C7 nonidentity cycles have no fixed points. The correction changes wording,
not the original masks, fitted predictors or reported values.
Code locations: studies/compact_context_v1/compact.py lines 497-507;
studies/compact_context_v1/context.py lines 189-216. These files are unchanged.
This issue was already present in 0.4.2 and was not introduced by 0.4.3.

Assessment after revision: independent contexts
-----------------------------------------------
No new experiment is required to support the revised descriptive findings
within the declared fixed panel. The clearer paper still does not establish
generalization to independently authored context families. Presentation alone
does not settle the importance or originality question for an ICML main-track
submission. If further scientific work is chosen, independent context
validation is the preferred next step, rather than another full replay or
calibration sweep. This is a recommendation, not an approved or executed run.

One focused study could freeze its source code, independently authored context
families, model/readout panel, construction/selection rules, several coherent
registrations, one primary contrast and uncertainty unit before evaluation.
A common complete source/action grid and common training-energy denominator
would compare registration-specific targets without different D4 masks. This
is a new scoring contract, not a reinterpretation of old means. Development,
selection and independent evaluation must be separated. Report all planned
registrations and model cells, including unfavorable outcomes. Do not turn
selected conjugates into an exchangeable permutation null or choose a
favorable comparison after inspecting the new evaluation.

Such a study can address contextual generalization and preference among the
selected registrations. It cannot alone exclude category coding, establish
semantic uniqueness or native causal use, or guarantee publication. The
scientific stops in the existing protocols remain unchanged. No experiment
code, cloud job or new evaluation data was created for this revision.
